# SEC-3F-001 race-relative last-3F history

Date: 2026-08-31 JST  
Status: preregistered and implemented; real-data cache and rolling model run not started

## Hypothesis

Past last-3F performance may contain running-content information that ordinal
finish, winner-relative full-race time gap, and the existing ratings do not
fully preserve. SEC-3F-001 adds exactly one column:

```text
sectional__decay_90d__mean_last_3f_speed_percentile
```

It does not add absolute sectional seconds, a field-median difference, passing
position, inferred style, pace interaction, distance/surface split, opponent
future result, model parameter, or LambdaRank-label change.

## Frozen transformation

For each historical JRA flat race, eligible observations are starters with a
finite `last_3f_seconds`. Let `n_valid` be their count. If `n_valid >= 2`, rank
last-3F seconds ascending with average ranks for ties and define the horse's own
observation as

\[
s_{i,r}=1-\frac{\operatorname{rank}_{i,r}-1}{n_{valid,r}-1}.
\]

Thus the uniquely fastest value is 1 and the uniquely slowest value is 0. Tied
values share their average-rank percentile. Non-starters and missing/infinite
sectionals do not enter either the rank or denominator. A race with fewer than
two valid sectionals supplies no observations.

The observation belongs only to that horse. Finish, margin, full-race time,
opponent rating, opponent later performance, and odds are unused. Absolute
seconds never cross race boundaries.

For target date `d`, the candidate is the 90-day half-life decayed mean of that
horse's observations from prior dates:

```text
weight(r, d) = 2 ** (-(d - date_r) / 90)
candidate = sum(weight * s) / sum(weight)
```

All target rows on a date are emitted before any observation from that date
updates state. A horse with no eligible prior observation remains `NaN`; zero
and the field mean are not used as cold-start imputations.

The exact executable transformation is
`configs/features/sec_3f_001.json`. Its canonical SHA-256 is frozen as:

```text

```

## Audit basis and selection boundary

PV-00 established that last 3F is present and numeric for 515,980 of 515,983
numeric finishers in the 2013--2023 raw audit, with at least two valid values in
every audited race. On the 2014--2021 train-only period, finish percentile and
last-3F percentile had Spearman `.7361`, while the winner was fastest in only
`37.60%` of races. This supports a separate within-race representation rather
than treating last 3F as finish or comparing absolute seconds across conditions.

Those diagnostics froze one representation only: ascending average tie rank,
the `[0,1]` formula above, 90-day half-life, and cold `NaN`. No target model
metric, 2024 result, 2025 result, final odds, or popularity was used to select
the formula.

## Cache and leakage contract

The raw-cache builder:

1. verifies the approved raw fingerprint;
2. removes 2025+ raw rows before normalization or result parsing;
3. uses 2013 as forward-state warmup;
4. emits every flat starter through 2024 so a valid cold-history `NaN` remains
   distinguishable from a failed join;
5. updates only from finite own past sectionals and batches every date;
6. accepts an arbitrary baseline cache, preserving all existing feature names,
   order, values, dtype behavior, and `NaN` positions;
7. appends only the frozen `float32` column and leaves all 2025 rows missing;
8. refuses overwrite and records baseline/config/transformation hashes.

The separate cache-control function verifies runner identity, old schema and
values, the one-column sectional contract, and the 2025 firewall. The
`sectional__` prefix is a closed model allowlist group; raw `上がり3F` remains
forbidden as a direct model feature.

## Decision rule

Judge Binary and LambdaRank separately. Positive differences mean improvement.

- Probability path: paired four-year macro Log Loss improvement interval lower
  bound above zero; Brier point improvement at least `-.001`, NDCG at least
  `-.002`, and Top-1 at least `-.005`.
- Ranking path: paired four-year macro NDCG improvement interval lower bound
  above zero; Log Loss point improvement at least `-.002`, Brier at least
  `-.001`, and Top-1 at least `-.005`.
- The path's primary metric must improve in at least three of four evaluation
  years.

Passing either complete path marks that family `accept` for a separately
authorized 2024 milestone. If no path passes and a guardrail fails or a primary
interval is wholly adverse, mark `reject`; otherwise mark `inconclusive`.
