# OPP-RECENT-001 opponent-only recent field strength

Date: 2026-08-31 JST  
Status: preregistered and implemented; real-data cache and rolling model run not started

## Hypothesis

The existing feature named
`horse_history__career__mean_opponent_elo` is not opponent-only. The baseline
computes the mean pre-race Elo over all active starters, including the target
horse, and then takes a career mean of those observations. A recent history
that excludes the target horse may represent the value of racing against
strong fields more directly.

OPP-RECENT-001 adds exactly one column:

```text
opponent_recent__decay_90d__mean_opponent_only_pre_elo
```

No career difference, top-opponent statistic, surface split, result margin,
feature interaction, model parameter, or LambdaRank label change is included.

## Frozen transformation

For each historical JRA flat race, use the corrected baseline's global ordinal
pairwise Elo before that race:

- initial rating `1500`
- `K=24`
- scale `400`
- active starters only; scratches and exclusions are not in the denominator
- same-date batch update

For starter (i) in a field of (n\ge2), freeze the post-race historical
observation as

\[
o_{i,r}=\frac{\sum_{j\in r} e_{j,r}^{pre}-e_{i,r}^{pre}}{n-1}.
\]

The observation uses neither horse (i)'s result in that race nor an
opponent's future result. The target feature is the exponentially decayed mean
of the horse's prior (o_{i,r}) observations with half-life 90 days. A horse
without history remains `NaN`; missing is not encoded as 1500 or zero.

Every row on a date is emitted before any observation from that date updates
state. Thus even a duplicated fixture in which a horse appears twice on one
date cannot see the other same-date race.

The executable payload is
`configs/features/opp_recent_001.json`. Its canonical transformation SHA-256
is frozen as:

```text
801e2a282629d4951af5d4c770a78f56f6e10c1311b76f1e5d6ba68cbdf7d940
```

## Train-only audit

Only 2014--2021 was used to audit the candidate representation; 2013 supplied
warmup state. Raw SHA-256 was
`270923ce73c4441e64173f242a8719de7d1e9b205508140463ca547ef7b1ca87`.

- Raw 2013--2021 rating stream: 425,908 starters in 29,909 flat races.
- Minimum observed starter count was 5; no one-starter real race occurred.
- All 360,318 strict 2014--2021 model rows joined to the raw-derived history.
- Raw-derived pre-Elo and cached float32 Elo differed by at most
  `0.000061035`; this is storage rounding.
- The self-excluded observation minus the inclusive field mean had standard
  deviation `1.90394`, 1st/99th percentiles `-5.11201/+5.31046`, and maximum
  absolute difference `36.11853` Elo points.
- The emitted recent feature was nonmissing for 324,465/360,318 rows
  (`90.0496%`). Annual coverage ranged from `89.1574%` to `90.6319%`.
- Its Spearman correlation with the existing self-inclusive career mean was
  `.97531`. Incremental value may therefore be small; this is evidence for a
  strict one-column test, not a reason to add variants.

No target prediction metric, 2024 result, 2025 result, odds, or popularity was
used to choose the formula.

## Cache and leakage contract

The builder reads the approved raw only after fingerprint verification. It
removes rows after 2024 before normalization and constructs the complete
forward rating stream rather than deriving history from the strict model
cache. The latter would omit 2013 warmup and active horses from historical
races containing a scratch or exclusion.

The augmented-cache builder accepts an arbitrary baseline cache and must:

1. preserve every existing feature name, order, value, and `NaN` position;
2. append only the frozen OPP-RECENT column as `float32`;
3. use a presence marker so a legitimate cold-history `NaN` is distinct from
   a missing join row;
4. cover every model row dated through 2024;
5. leave every 2025+ row without a history join or feature value;
6. refuse overwrite and record baseline/config/transformation hashes.

Primary leakage risks fixed by tests are post-race rather than pre-race Elo,
self-inclusive means, same-day sequential state, inclusion of scratches in the
denominator, use of later opponent results, strict-cache-only reconstruction,
and normalization of 2025 outcomes.

## Decision rule

Judge Binary and LambdaRank separately. Positive differences always mean the
candidate improved.

- Probability path: the paired four-year macro Log Loss improvement interval
  must have lower bound above zero; Brier point improvement must be at least
  `-.001`, NDCG at least `-.002`, and Top-1 at least `-.005`.
- Ranking path: the paired four-year macro NDCG improvement interval must have
  lower bound above zero; Log Loss point improvement must be at least `-.002`,
  Brier at least `-.001`, and Top-1 at least `-.005`.
- The path's primary metric must improve in at least three of four evaluation
  years. This direction rule is additional to, not a replacement for, the
  paired block interval.

Passing either complete path marks that model-family candidate `accept` for an
important 2024 milestone request. If no path passes and a guardrail fails or a
primary interval is wholly adverse, mark `reject`; otherwise mark
`inconclusive`. An accept in one family does not imply accept in the other.
This preregistration does not itself open 2024, and 2025 remains prohibited for
iterative selection.
