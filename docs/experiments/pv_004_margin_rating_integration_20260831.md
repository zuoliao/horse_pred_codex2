# PV-04 Margin-aware rating score in LightGBM

Status: preregistered after PV-03 and before building the augmented cache or running
the candidate LightGBM models.

## Hypothesis and arms

The frozen margin-aware Elo pre-race raw score may summarize persistent opponent- and
margin-aware strength that is not fully represented by PV-01's 90-day signed time-gap
mean or the existing ordinal history features.

| Arm | Features |
|---|---:|
| Primary control | PV-01 lean history + signed time gap, 254 columns |
| Candidate | Primary control + `margin_rating__score_pre`, 255 columns |

Only one numeric feature is added. The score is generated by the exact PV-03
candidate state update: global pairwise Elo, initial 1500, K=48, scale=200,
time-margin logistic actual, `tau=.125 sec/1000m`, no surface blend, and same-day
batching. The 2023-fitted standalone temperature and calibrated probability are not
model inputs. R6's starts, uncertainty and raw-probability fields are also excluded.

## Cache and PIT gates

- Cut raw rows at 2024 before normalization; 2025 receives no feature value.
- Generate the rating once from 2013 warm-up through 2024 in chronological order.
- Join by `(race_id, horse_id)` one-to-one to the existing PV-01 cache.
- Preserve every existing row, feature value, NaN position and column order exactly;
  append one float32 feature and update only the ignored cache sidecar.
- Reproduce the PV-03 candidate's 2024 raw scores on the common population.
- Rerun the 254-column control from the augmented cache and require exact predictions,
  iterations and metrics versus the existing PV-01 control artifact before candidate
  evaluation.

Any failed gate stops the experiment.

## Frozen model and temporal protocol

Both model families retain their existing configs and seed. LightGBM fits 2014–2021,
uses 2022 for early stopping, fits its output temperature on 2023, and is compared
once on the same strict 2024 population. 2025, odds and ROI are unused.

The paired comparison uses 10,000 four-date moving-block draws with seed 20240830.
Binary and LambdaRank are judged separately using the unchanged PV-01 paths.

Probability path:

- Log Loss point improvement at least .002 and paired 95% lower bound above zero.
- Brier point non-worse.
- NDCG@3 at least -.002 and Top-1 at least -.005.

Ranking path:

- NDCG@3 paired 95% lower bound above zero.
- Log Loss at least -.002, Brier at least -.001, and Top-1 at least -.005.

The candidate passes a family if either complete path passes. Because the conservative
LambdaRank incumbent is the lean 253-column model rather than PV-01's inconclusive
254-column candidate, LambdaRank is promoted only if the 255-column candidate passes
the applicable path both against PV-01 254 and against lean 253. Passing only one
comparison is reported but does not establish a new incumbent.

Feature correlations, availability, gain importance and condition slices are
diagnostics only. A high correlation alone cannot reject the feature, and feature
importance alone cannot accept it. Any supported result remains subject to 2026+
prospective confirmation and makes no betting-return claim.

## Result

All cache gates passed: 533,853 rows and all 269 old cache features were exact, the
new score covered all 489,674 pre-2025 scoring rows, 2025 nonmissing count was zero,
and the fresh 2024 score reproduced PV-03 with maximum difference zero. The
254-feature control also reproduced both PV-01 model predictions, iterations,
temperatures and metrics exactly.

Primary 2024 comparison against PV-01:

| Family | Candidate metrics (NDCG / Top-1 / LL / Brier) | Improvement (NDCG / Top-1 / LL / Brier) |
|---|---|---|
| Binary | .497594 / .294002 / 2.078758 / .828107 | +.000029 / -.002458 / -.000087 / -.000434 |
| LambdaRank | .496211 / .294166 / 2.077296 / .828483 | +.002079 / +.003605 / +.000536 / -.000379 |

Paired primary intervals:

- Binary NDCG `+.000029 [-.005236,+.005211]`; Log Loss
  `-.000087 [-.005610,+.005347]`.
- LambdaRank NDCG `+.002079 [-.002779,+.007385]`; Log Loss
  `+.000536 [-.005335,+.005874]`.

Neither family passed the probability or ranking path against PV-01, so the one
absolute margin-rating score has **no demonstrated incremental value** and is not
adopted. This is an inconclusive near-null comparison, not evidence of material harm.

The fixed LambdaRank comparison against the conservative lean 253-column incumbent
did pass the probability path: NDCG +.003778 `[-.001619,+.009187]`, Log Loss +.007366
`[+.000585,+.014005]`, Brier +.001465, and Top-1 +.006064. However, PV-01 already
accounts for most of this combined improvement; because PV-04 did not pass its
primary 255-versus-254 comparison, the preregistered rule does not promote it as a
new incumbent.

The added feature nevertheless took 2.33% of Binary gain (rank 8/255) and 4.10% of
LambdaRank gain (rank 5/255). High importance without out-of-sample improvement is
consistent with substitution for existing features. Its Spearman correlation with
the existing ordinal Elo was .969 on 2014–2021 and about .971 in 2022/2024; correlation
with PV-01 was .738 on train and .698 in 2024. The absolute score is therefore highly
redundant. A separately preregistered delta-versus-same-spec-ordinal representation
is a more targeted next test; it must not be treated as part of PV-04.

`experiments/race_content_20260831/pv_004_summary.json`.
