## Hypothesis

A fixed equal-weight arithmetic blend of the coherent Binary and LambdaRank
race probabilities improves race Log Loss consistently over both singleton
references. ENS-01 changes no model, feature, target, HPO parameter, blend
weight, or betting rule.

HPO-01 retained the default Binary parameters and rejected its Ranker HPO

- LambdaRank: `lambdarank_candidate`, lean plus accepted SEC-3F, 254 features,
  default parameters.

## Fixed blend and independent calibration

For runner `i` in a race, let `qB_i` and `qR_i` be the stored T=1 coherent
probabilities, not the already calibrated singleton probabilities. ENS-01
defines exactly one candidate:

```text
q0_i = 0.5 * qB_i + 0.5 * qR_i
s_i  = log(clip(q0_i, 1e-15, 1))
qENS_i(T) = softmax_race(s_i / T)
```

Because both inputs sum to one, `q0` is coherent. At T=1,
`softmax(log(q0))` must reproduce `q0` within `1e-12`; otherwise execution
aborts. `log(q0)`, not binary logit, is the ensemble utility.

Each fold receives one new ensemble temperature fitted only from that fold's
calibration-role winner labels. Singleton references retain their own stored
`probability_calibrated` for Log Loss/Brier and their own stored `utility` for
NDCG/Top-1. The ensemble uses its independently calibrated probability and
`log(q0)` ranking utility. This avoids feeding two already calibrated inputs
through a third calibration fit on the same year.

The arithmetic form and weights are fixed. Weight-search candidate count is
zero. Log-space pooling, alternative weights, and blending raw utilities are
not inspected in ENS-01.

## Temporal gate

The fixed candidate is first screened on the 2020, 2021, and 2022 evaluation
folds. Against **each** singleton it must have:

- year-macro race Log Loss improvement at least `.002`;
- positive Log Loss improvement in at least two of three years;
- race Brier improvement at least `0`;
- NDCG@3 improvement at least `-.002`;
- Top-1 improvement at least `-.005`.

Failure against either reference rejects the ensemble. Because the blend is a
single fixed candidate rather than a selected weight, the runner still records
the separately reported single 2023 confirmation fold; its result cannot
override a failed multi-year screen. If the screen fails, the runner does not
construct, score, or save 2023 ensemble predictions; confirmation remains
explicitly `not_opened`.

In 2023 the ensemble must pass the same path against **both** singleton
references: Log Loss point improvement at least `.002`, paired Log Loss 95%
interval lower bound strictly above zero, and all point guardrails above. Both
comparisons must pass to accept. A guardrail failure or wholly negative Log
Loss interval rejects; otherwise the result is inconclusive. Only accept may
replace singleton use; inconclusive or reject retains the separate models.

Both stages use paired moving four-date blocks, 10,000 resamples, seed
`20240830`, and 95% percentile intervals. The run records two candidate-
reference comparisons, no weight search, and the accumulated lower-bound
selection-use inventory. These years have prior research exposure and are not
untouched holdouts. The intervals condition on stored model predictions and
do not include model-refit uncertainty.

## Result

The run used clean commit
`df5c8c9c6b9c123d58e3966ee9efaa0ac39a0e11`, authenticated the frozen
SEC-3F source hashes, fitted no LightGBM model, searched zero weights, and
used zero 2024/2025 rows and no odds.

On the 2020--2022 screen, the blend clearly improved probability quality over
the SEC-3F LambdaRank reference: Log Loss `+.01537`
`[+.01237,+.01833]` and Brier `+.00286` `[+.00198,+.00373]`, with Log Loss
improving in all three years.

Against the stronger Binary PV-01 reference, the blend improved NDCG in all
three years (`+.00112`) and Brier in all three (`+.00094`
`[+.00037,+.00152]`). However, Log Loss improved by only `+.00157`
`[-.00070,+.00387]`, below the frozen `+.002` minimum, and improved in two of
three years. It therefore failed the requirement to clearly beat both
singletons on the same probability path.

The decision is `reject`. The runner did not construct or save any 2023
ensemble prediction, and confirmation is `not_opened`. Do not tune the blend
weight or choose another blend form from these results. Tracked evidence is
`experiments/ensemble_20260831/summary.json`; complete local artifacts are
under `artifacts/ens_01_fixed_5050_20260831/`.
